Our story

Family-run, fire-fed, and proudly local.

What started as a tiny corner kitchen is now the table your neighbors gather around for birthdays, first dates, and ordinary Tuesdays that deserve something better.

We source from farms within 50 miles, cook everything over live fire, and treat every guest like they're eating in our own home.
